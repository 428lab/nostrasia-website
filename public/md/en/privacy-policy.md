# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 20, 2024

Our website respects the privacy of our users and is committed to protecting their personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and protection of personal information on our website.

## 1. Collection of Personal Information

Our website uses Google Forms for pre-registration for free events. During pre-registration, we collect the following information:

- Optional: Nostr npub
- Required: Email address

## 2. Purpose of Use of Personal Information

The collected personal information will be used for the following purposes:

- Providing advance information about the event
- Event check-in on the day of the event
- Providing information about future events

## 3. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

Except as required by law, our website does not provide personal information of users to third parties.

## 4. Correction and Suspension of Use of Personal Information

If users wish to correct, add, delete, or suspend the use of their personal information, please contact us through this inquiry form. Our website will respond promptly within a reasonable scope.

## 5. Use of Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to collect user statistics and help improve the site and enhance our services. Data collected by Google Analytics is anonymized and cannot be used to identify individuals. For more information about Google Analytics, please refer to this [link](https://www.google.com/analytics/).

## 6. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through this [inquiry form](contact_form_link).

## 7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised without prior notice. The revised Privacy Policy will become effective once posted on our website.
